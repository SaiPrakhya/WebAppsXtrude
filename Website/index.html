About XtrudeInc

XtrudeInc is a startup associated with 3D Printing and Design. Focused on the production of 3D printed customizable products and providing innovative solutions to individuals and businesses alike.

From concept to creation, we are dedicated towards delivering high-quality 3D printed products that cater to our customers' lifestyle and accessory needs.

Our Services

The services we provide at XtrudeInc include 3D product rendering, visual prototyping as well as physical product prototyping and, rapid prototyping services for businesses and individuals who are looking to transition their abstract ideas into visual and real life models.

Custom orders

As a startup that understands the value of uniqueness in every design and product, we offer our customers the option to place customized order preferences in order to meet with their style and preferences of an ideal product in their vision. All you have to do is send us an email or simply reach out to us on the available platforms and detail your product requirements with us.

Our Mission

At XtrudeInc, our mission is to provide high quality 3D printed products that cater to the unique needs and preferences of our customers. On top of that, we also aim to deliver quality prototyping services as well as 3D visual product rendering services for our beloved clientele who are looking to explore the opportunity of bringing their ideas to life.

In the long run, we wish to expand our services towards exploring several other use cases in the current rising trend of manufacturing.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become one of the leading providers of 3D printing & design, prototyping & small-scale manufacturing, as well as providing innovative 3D solutions for our clients in various industries, specifically in the domain of medtech and education.

As an extension to our product line, we wish to explore new possibilities in the field of 3D software solutions for businesses.

Our Products in 3D

Take a look at our products in 3D.

Single framed lithophane
This product consists of a 3D printed single photo frame lithophane.

Upon illuminating it against a backlight of any source, the caricature will light up to display a beautiful projection of the image engraved on the surface.

This product variant under lithophanes comes with limited customization benefits and the light source for this product must be arranged by the customer for usage.

Basic enclosure lithophane
This variant offers 4 frames allowing the customer to choose upto 4 pictures for their product.

When it comes to customization, the color of the base of the enclosure can be chosen by the user within a series of several available colors.

A light source with batteries will be provided along with the product during delivery.

Named enclosure lithophane
Similar to the basic version of the enclosure lithophane product, this variant allows you to customize the base of the enclosure as well as engraving your name or any text on top of its surface.

However, the text can be engraved only on one side of the enclosure while keeping all the other sides of the base empty.

A light source with batteries will be provided along with the product during delivery

Named enclosure lithophane
This product is similar to the previous named lithophane product.

Similar to the previous named lithophane product, this version lets you have your name or any text engraved on all sides of the base of the enclosure.

Along with that, customizable options are available, you can choose the color of the base within a series of available colors as well as the color of text and the text itself.

Just like all the other enclosure variants, you can choose upto 4 images for the enclosure.

A light source along with batteries will be provided to you during delivery of the product.

The Founders

Sai Prakhya is the Co-Founder & CEO of XtrudeInc.
As a kid who grew up with the idea of doing things differently, He was lucky enough to experience the several pleasures of life that his childhood could offer. As an observant kid, he drew most of his abstract ideas by inspiring them from movies and video games, It was fun. From this mindset alone, he always wanted to do things more differently than others.

In pursuit of this possibility, by making stupid mistakes and learning lessons along the way, he would become fascinated with the concept of turning virtual and abstract frameworks into real-life models. In other words, 3D printing.

After discovering the potential of 3D printing and it's rising number of use cases along it's evolution, He set out to launch his very first startup, XtrudeInc along with his college benchmate, Surya Sudhanshu.

Surya Sudhanshu, is the Co-Founder & CMO of XtrudeInc.
Fascinated by latest trends in the consumer technology industry, Sudhanshu would spend every waking day during his schooling learning about tech. The idea of spreading information about the things that he was learning grew of interest.

He would often isolate himself and spend countless hours learning about consumer technology and soon enough, He would begin writing blogs and articles on them. Later on, he began commercializing his writing and marketing skills that led him to establish a self-sustaining lifestyle for his age.

As a believer in the idea of lifelong learning and as someone who has equally great ambitions as his partner, he found himself on the bandwagon of building XtrudeInc by heading the marketing aspect of the company, officially posing as the CMO.
