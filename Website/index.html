About XtrudeInc

XtrudeInc is a startup specializing in the domain of 3D printing and 3D design. We are dedicated to offering unique Products catered to a range of customers in various domains, including home decor, lifestyle accessories, and more. We also specialize in providing custom 3D-Product rendering services for businesses or individuals looking to bring their ideas to life.
















Objectives

As our objectives expand over time, initially, we aim to expand our operations towards provision offull-fledged 3D printing services for customers looking to explore the 3D printing space in manufacturing or for rapid-prototyping.

Our Mission

Our mission is to introduce unique products into consumer-marketplaces that cater to lifestyle needs of our beloved customers. At the same time, we aim to provide an opportunity for businesses and individuals to explore the 3D-design and additive manufacturing landscape in order to align with their goals and aspirations.





Our Vision

Many more years down the line, we envision XtrudeInc to be a leading name in the 3D-designing and additive manufacturing industry, known for its strategy to combine technology with 3D-design and provision of exceptional services as well as innovative products that enhances the quality of operations in several domains and equally enhances the lives of it's customers.
