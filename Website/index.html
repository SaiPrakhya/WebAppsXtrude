About XtrudeInc

XtrudeInc is a startup associated with 3D Printing and Design. Focused on the production of 3D printed customizable products and providing innovative solutions to individuals and businesses alike.

From concept to creation, we are dedicated towards delivering high-quality 3D printed products that cater to our customers' lifestyle and accessory needs.

Our Services

The services we provide at XtrudeInc include 3D product rendering, visual prototyping as well as physical product prototyping and, rapid prototyping services for businesses and individuals who are looking to transition their abstract ideas into visual and real life models.

Custom orders

As a startup that understands the value of uniqueness in every design and product, we offer our customers the option to place customized order preferences in order to meet with their style and preferences of an ideal product in their vision. All you have to do is send us an email or simply reach out to us on the available platforms and detail your product requirements with us.

Our Mission

At XtrudeInc, our mission is to provide high quality 3D printed products that cater to the unique needs and preferences of our customers. On top of that, we also aim to deliver quality prototyping services as well as 3D visual product rendering services for our beloved clientele who are looking to explore the opportunity of bringing their ideas to life.

In the long run, we wish to expand our services towards exploring several other use cases in the current rising trend of manufacturing.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become one of the leading providers of 3D printing & design, prototyping & small-scale manufacturing, as well as providing innovative 3D solutions for our clients in various industries, specifically in the domain of medtech and education.

As an extension to our product line, we wish to explore new possibilities in the field of 3D software solutions for businesses.
