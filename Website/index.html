About XtrudeInc

XtrudeInc is a startup that is associated with the field of 3D printing and 3D design. Based out of Bengaluru in India, XtrudeInc has it's own product line of lifestyle products catering to their clientele's lifestyle accessory needs. On top of that, they also strive to provide services in 3D printing, rapid prototyping as well as 3D design and virtual product rendering services to businesses and individuals alike.










Our Services

Our Services include rapid prototyping, custom designs for our products, small-scale product designing as well as virtual product rendering.

Reach out to us via our socials or contact details if you would like to explore our services.










More Services

On top of our primary services, we also take on contracts involving small-scale manufacturing for your business including designing and prototyping.

For our product line, we also accept custom orders in bulk catered to your preferences or requirements accordingly.










Our Mission

At XtrudeInc, our mission is to provide high quality 3D printed products that cater to the unique needs and preferences of our customers. On top of that, we also aim to deliver quality prototyping services as well as 3D visual product rendering services for our beloved clientele who are looking to explore the opportunity of bringing their ideas to life. In the long run, we wish to expand our services towards exploring several other use cases in the current rising trend of manufacturing.










Our Vision

Our vision is to become one of the leading providers of 3D printing & design, prototyping & small-scale manufacturing, as well as providing innovative 3D solutions for our clients in various industries, specifically in the domain of medtech and education. As an extension to our product line, we wish to explore new possibilities in the field of 3D software solutions for businesses.

Our Products in 3D

View our products in 3D.



Single framed lithophane
This product consists of a 3D printed single photo frame lithophane. Upon illuminating it against a backlight of any source, the caricature lights up to display a beautiful projection of the image engraved on the surface. This variant comes with limited customization; the light source must be arranged by the customer.

Basic enclosure lithophane
Offers four frames for up to four pictures. You can choose the color of the enclosure base from several options. A light source with batteries is included with delivery.

Named enclosure lithophane
Like the basic enclosure, plus custom name or text engraved on the top surface (one side only). Light source with batteries included.

Named enclosure lithophane
Similar to the previous product but allows text on all sides of the base. Choose the base color, text color, and up to four images for the enclosure. Light source with batteries included.
